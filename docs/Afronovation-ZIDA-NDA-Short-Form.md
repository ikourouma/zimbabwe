# Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement (Short Form)

*Afronovation | Zimbabwe Digital Investment & Economic Intelligence Platform*
*Draft for legal review | Not for execution until reviewed by counsel | September 2026*

> **Read this before circulating.** This is a short-form instrument, built to be signed in a room ahead of a platform demonstration — not a substitute for full legal review. A complete, clause-by-clause version with definitions, schedules and drafting notes for counsel is kept at [Afronovation-ZIDA-Non-Disclosure-Agreement.md](Afronovation-ZIDA-Non-Disclosure-Agreement.md). Where the two differ, the long-form agreement governs; this short form exists so a stakeholder can read, understand and sign it in the time available before a demonstration, rather than as the final word on any point it compresses. Items requiring a decision before signature are marked in square brackets.

## 1. Parties and purpose

This Agreement is between **Afronovation, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Cary, North Carolina, USA ("**Afronovation**"), and **the Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency** ("**ZIDA**"), a statutory body of the Republic of Zimbabwe. Each is a "**Party**."

Afronovation has built a software platform for digital investment promotion and economic intelligence, configured for Zimbabwe and operated at `zidaproject.com` (the "**Platform**"). The Parties wish to explore making the Platform available to ZIDA and the Government of Zimbabwe (the "**Purpose**"). Each Party will disclose confidential information to the other in the course of that exploration. **This Agreement must be signed before any demonstration of the Platform, any credential is issued, or any documentation describing it is shared.** Signing it creates no obligation on either Party to proceed further.

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means anything either Party discloses to the other in connection with the Purpose that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including without limitation:

- the Platform itself — its source code, architecture, data model, workflows, entitlement/permission model, user interface and design system, and any documentation or demonstration of it;
- pricing, licensing, commercial terms and business plans;
- official ZIDA/Government data — project information, financial indicators, feasibility material, institutional records and policy materials; and
- the existence and content of these discussions themselves.

**It does not include** information the receiving Party can show was already lawfully known to it without restriction, is or becomes public other than through its own breach, is lawfully received from a third party free to share it, or is independently developed without reference to it. **The Platform is reachable on the public internet — being able to see its front end does not put its source code, architecture, data model or design outside this definition.** What can be seen is not what has been disclosed.

## 3. What each Party agrees to do with it

Whoever receives Confidential Information agrees to:

1. keep it strictly confidential and use it only to evaluate the Purpose — for no other reason;
2. share it only with its own people, affiliates, or professional advisers who genuinely need it and are themselves bound to confidentiality, and remain responsible for anything any of them does with it;
3. protect it with reasonable care, at least as much as it gives its own confidential material;
4. tell the other Party promptly — within five business days — if it becomes aware of any unauthorised access, use or disclosure; and
5. never disclose it to a competitor of the other Party, or to anyone building or procuring a competing platform, under any circumstances.

If a Party is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information (court order, regulator, statute), it may do so only after giving the other Party prompt notice where the law allows, and disclosing only what is legally required.

## 4. Protecting the Platform specifically

Because the Platform is Afronovation's core asset, ZIDA additionally agrees that neither it nor anyone it shares access with will, without Afronovation's prior written consent:

- copy, reproduce or recreate the Platform or any part of it;
- reverse-engineer, decompile or otherwise try to derive its source code, architecture or data model;
- use anything learned from access to it to design, build, specify or procure a competing or substantially similar platform;
- publish, present, screenshot, record or demonstrate it to a third party;
- attempt to bypass its access controls, run a security test against it, or scrape/bulk-download its data; or
- share an access credential issued to a named individual with anyone else.

Data shown in any demonstration environment is illustrative and not validated official data, and must not be relied upon or presented as official.

## 5. Ownership

Afronovation owns the Platform and everything that makes it up, before, during and after this Agreement — nothing here transfers, licenses or gives ZIDA any right in it beyond evaluating it for the Purpose. ZIDA and the Government of Zimbabwe own their own data — Afronovation acquires no rights in it beyond what is needed to operate the Platform for the Purpose, and will treat it as ZIDA's Confidential Information throughout. Neither Party may challenge the other's ownership of what is confirmed above.

## 6. Term and what survives it

This Agreement runs for **three years** from signature, endable earlier by either Party on **30 days'** written notice. The confidentiality obligations above survive for **five years** from each disclosure — and **indefinitely** for source code, system architecture, and anything that remains a trade secret. On request or at the end of this Agreement, the receiving Party will stop using, and return or securely delete, all Confidential Information within 30 days (routine backups excepted, and still bound by this Agreement for as long as they're retained).

## 7. If this Agreement is breached

Both Parties agree that a breach of confidentiality or of the Platform protections in Section 4 could cause harm that money alone cannot fix, so the wronged Party may seek an **immediate court injunction** to stop it, without having to prove specific financial loss or post a bond, in addition to any other remedy available. Whoever wins an enforcement action can recover its reasonable legal costs. Nothing here limits protection under applicable trade secret law, including the whistleblower protection in 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) for reporting a suspected violation of law to a government official in confidence.

## 9. A few standing terms

This Agreement, once signed, is the entire understanding between the Parties on this subject and replaces any earlier one. It can only be changed in writing, signed by both sides. Neither Party may assign it without the other's consent (except Afronovation may do so to an affiliate or in a merger/sale of its business). Notices go in writing to the addresses below. This Agreement is in English, and that text governs. Nothing in it creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship between the Parties.
