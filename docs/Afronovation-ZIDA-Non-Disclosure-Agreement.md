## 2. Purpose and Background

Afronovation has designed, developed and owns a proprietary software platform for digital investment promotion and economic intelligence, configured for Zimbabwe and operated at the internet domain `zidaproject.com` (the "**Platform**").

The Parties wish to explore a possible engagement under which the Platform would be made available to ZIDA and the Government of Zimbabwe under a licensing, implementation and support arrangement (the "**Purpose**").

For the Purpose, each Party will disclose to the other information that is confidential, proprietary or commercially sensitive. Afronovation will disclose the Platform itself, together with its architecture, functionality, design, methodology and supporting documentation. ZIDA may disclose official data, project information, institutional priorities, policy materials and procurement information.

This Agreement governs the treatment of that information. It is a precondition to any demonstration of the Platform, to the issue of any access credentials, and to the delivery of any documentation describing the Platform.

**No engagement is created by this Agreement.** It imposes obligations of confidence and protects intellectual property. It does not commit either Party to enter into any commercial arrangement.

## 4. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, before or after the Effective Date, in any form and by any means, whether or not marked or identified as confidential, which a reasonable person in the position of the Receiving Party would understand to be confidential or proprietary given its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

**(a) Platform and technical information.** The Platform and all Platform Material as defined in clause 10 and particularised in Schedule B; source code, object code and scripts; system, data and security architecture; database schemas and data models; application programming interfaces and integration designs; algorithms, workflow logic and state machines; entitlement and authorisation models; user interface designs, design systems, visual language and interaction patterns; infrastructure, hosting and deployment configuration; test data, test plans and quality assurance material; and any credentials, access tokens or connection strings.

**(b) Commercial information.** Pricing, licensing models, fee structures, cost information, commercial terms, proposals, business plans, financial projections, funding arrangements, and the terms and existence of any negotiations between the Parties.

**(c) Methodological information.** Implementation methodologies, delivery frameworks, programme structures, governance models, maturity assessments, and the National Digital Acceleration Program framework and all its constituent materials.

**(d) Documentation.** Concept notes, platform documentation, walkthrough guides, persona guides, technical specifications, roadmaps, presentations, demonstrations and any recording of them.

**(e) Government and institutional information.** Official data, investment project information, financial indicators, feasibility material, project readiness assessments, institutional records, policy materials, procurement information, and any information disclosed by ZIDA that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

**(f) Third-party information.** Information belonging to a third party that a Party is obliged to keep confidential.

**(g) Derived material.** Any note, analysis, compilation, study, summary, extract or Derivative Work prepared by a Receiving Party that contains, reflects or is derived from any of the foregoing.

The fact that discussions are taking place between the Parties, the subject matter of those discussions, and the existence and terms of this Agreement are themselves Confidential Information.

## 5. Exclusions From Confidential Information

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

**(a)** was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession, without obligation of confidence, before it was disclosed by the Disclosing Party;

**(b)** is or becomes publicly available other than through any act or omission of the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

**(c)** is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who is free to disclose it and is under no obligation of confidence to the Disclosing Party; or

**(d)** is independently developed by the Receiving Party by persons who have had no access to and no knowledge of the Confidential Information.

The following limitations apply to these exclusions, and are material:

**(i) No aggregate exclusion.** A combination of features, elements or information does not fall within an exclusion merely because individual features, elements or items of information fall within one. The combination itself must satisfy an exclusion.

**(ii) No partial exclusion.** Information does not fall within an exclusion merely because it is embraced by more general information that falls within one.

**(iii) Public visibility is not disclosure.** The Platform is operated on the public internet and portions of it are visible without authentication. **The public visibility of the Platform's front end does not place its architecture, source code, data model, workflow logic, entitlement model, design system or any other Platform Material within exclusion (b).** What can be seen is not what is disclosed.

## 6. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

**(a)** hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;

**(b)** use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose, and for no other purpose whatsoever;

**(c)** not disclose Confidential Information to any person except as permitted by clause 7 or required by clause 8;

**(d)** protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of comparable sensitivity, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;

**(e)** apply and maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the Confidential Information, including access controls, secure storage and secure transmission;

**(f)** limit access to those Authorised Recipients who have a genuine need to know for the Purpose;

**(g)** ensure that each Authorised Recipient is bound by obligations of confidence no less protective than those in this Agreement, and remain **fully responsible and liable** for any act or omission of any Authorised Recipient that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if committed by the Receiving Party;

**(h)** maintain a record of the Authorised Recipients to whom Confidential Information has been disclosed, and provide that record to the Disclosing Party on reasonable request;

**(i)** not remove, obscure or alter any proprietary notice, marking, watermark or attribution appearing on or in any Confidential Information; and

**(j)** notify the Disclosing Party in writing without undue delay, and in any event within **five (5) business days**, on becoming aware of any unauthorised access to, use of, or disclosure of Confidential Information, or any loss of material containing it, and cooperate fully in investigating and mitigating it.

## 7. Permitted Disclosures and Authorised Recipients

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to the following, and only to the extent necessary for the Purpose:

**(a)** its Representatives who have a genuine need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by written or professional obligations of confidence;

**(b)** its Affiliates, subject to the same conditions;

**(c)** in the case of ZIDA, officials of the Government of Zimbabwe and of ministries, agencies and public institutions listed in Schedule A, where their involvement is necessary to evaluate the Purpose, and provided each such official is informed of and observes the terms of this Agreement; and

**(d)** its professional legal and financial advisers, who are bound by professional duties of confidence.

The Receiving Party shall, before disclosure, inform each Authorised Recipient of the confidential nature of the Confidential Information and of the obligations under this Agreement.

**Disclosure to any competitor of the Disclosing Party, to any party engaged in developing or procuring a competing platform, or to any prospective alternative supplier, is prohibited absolutely and is not cured by the recipient signing a confidentiality undertaking.**

## 8. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required to disclose Confidential Information by law, by an order of a court or tribunal of competent jurisdiction, by a regulatory or governmental authority, or under the rules of a securities exchange, it may make that disclosure provided that it:

**(a)** gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice before the disclosure, unless prohibited from doing so by law, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy;

**(b)** provides reasonable cooperation, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in any effort by the Disclosing Party to obtain such an order or remedy;

**(c)** discloses only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose, on the written advice of counsel; and

**(d)** uses reasonable efforts to obtain assurances that the disclosed information will be treated confidentially.

Where ZIDA is subject to statutory access-to-information or public-records obligations, it shall notify Afronovation of any request that may require disclosure of Confidential Information and shall, so far as the applicable law permits, assert all available exemptions for trade secrets, commercially sensitive information and confidential third-party information.

Information disclosed under this clause remains Confidential Information for all other purposes.

## 10. Specific Platform Protections

This clause states obligations specific to the Platform. It is separate from clause 6 because the risks it addresses are specific, and general confidentiality language does not adequately capture them.

**10.1 Platform Material.** "**Platform Material**" means the Platform and every component and expression of it, including without limitation the categories particularised in **Schedule B**.

**10.2 Prohibited acts.** The Receiving Party shall not, and shall procure that its Representatives, Affiliates and Authorised Recipients do not, directly or indirectly:

**(a)** copy, reproduce, replicate or recreate the Platform or any Platform Material, in whole or in part, in any medium;

**(b)** create, commission, procure or assist in creating any Derivative Work of, or any product or system substantially derived from or based upon, the Platform or any Platform Material;

**(c)** reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, decrypt, or otherwise attempt to derive or reconstruct the source code, architecture, data model, algorithms or underlying structure of the Platform, by any means, whether or not such acts would otherwise be permitted by law, save to the extent that such a restriction is unenforceable under applicable mandatory law;

**(d)** use the Platform, any Platform Material, or any knowledge or understanding gained from access to them, to design, specify, procure, build, evaluate or benchmark any competing or substantially similar platform, product or system, whether by the Receiving Party, by an Affiliate, or by any third party;

**(e)** incorporate any part of the Platform Material, or any material derived from it, into any specification, requirements document, terms of reference, request for proposal, request for tender, or procurement document, whether or not attributed;

**(f)** disclose, publish, present or demonstrate the Platform or any Platform Material to any third party, including at any conference, exhibition or public forum, without the prior written consent of Afronovation;

**(g)** capture, record, photograph, screenshot or otherwise reproduce any part of the Platform interface, except where expressly permitted in writing by Afronovation and solely for the Purpose;

**(h)** access or attempt to access any part of the Platform beyond the entitlements granted to the credentials issued, or attempt to circumvent, disable or test any access control, authentication or authorisation mechanism;

**(i)** conduct any penetration test, vulnerability scan, load test or security assessment of the Platform without Afronovation's prior written consent and an agreed scope;

**(j)** extract, scrape, harvest or bulk-download data, content or structure from the Platform by automated means; or

**(k)** share, transfer or permit the use of any access credential issued for the Purpose, each of which is personal to the individual to whom it is issued.

**10.3 Credentials.** Access credentials issued for the Purpose are issued on the terms of this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall maintain a register of the individuals holding credentials and shall notify Afronovation without undue delay when any such individual ceases to require access. Afronovation may revoke any credential at any time.

**10.4 Demonstration environments.** Data present in any demonstration or evaluation environment is illustrative and is not validated official data. It shall not be relied upon, republished or represented as official.

**10.5 Independent development.** Nothing in this clause prevents ZIDA or the Government of Zimbabwe from independently procuring or developing investment promotion systems using personnel who have had no access to the Platform or to any Platform Material, and without use of or reference to any Confidential Information. The burden of demonstrating independent development rests with the Party asserting it, and clause 5's requirement of contemporaneous written records applies.

## 11. Government Data and National Content

**11.1 Definition.** "**Government Data**" means all data, content and records owned by or originating from the Government of Zimbabwe, ZIDA, any ministry, agency or public institution, including official investment project information, financial indicators, readiness assessments, institutional records, policy materials, documents uploaded to the Platform, and all national content.

**11.2 Ownership.** Government Data is and remains the property of the Government of Zimbabwe or the relevant institution. Afronovation acquires no ownership interest in it.

**11.3 Afronovation's obligations.** Afronovation shall treat Government Data as Confidential Information of ZIDA, shall use it only as necessary for the Purpose and for the operation of the Platform, shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under this Agreement, and shall not use it for any commercial purpose unrelated to the Purpose.

**11.4 Aggregated and anonymised data.** Afronovation may compile aggregated, anonymised and de-identified statistical information relating to platform performance, usage patterns and system operation for the purposes of operating, securing and improving the Platform, provided that such information does not identify Zimbabwe, ZIDA, any institution, any individual, any project, or any investor, and cannot reasonably be used to do so.

**11.5 Return and deletion.** On termination of this Agreement or on ZIDA's written request, Afronovation shall return or delete Government Data in accordance with clause 15, subject to the retention exceptions stated there.

**11.6 Personal data.** Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law in respect of any personal data disclosed under this Agreement, including the Data Protection Act [Chapter 11:12] of Zimbabwe and any applicable United States federal or state law. Where the Parties proceed to an engagement, they shall enter into such further data processing terms as applicable law requires.

## 13. Non-Circumvention and Non-Solicitation

**13.1 Non-circumvention.** During the term of this Agreement and for **two (2) years** after it ends, the Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information to circumvent the Disclosing Party by dealing directly with any business contact, partner, supplier, subcontractor or introduced third party whose identity or relevance was learned through Confidential Information, where the purpose or effect is to obtain for itself or a third party the benefit of the opportunity that is the subject of the Purpose.

**13.2 Non-solicitation.** During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** after it ends, neither Party shall solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of the other Party with whom it had material contact in connection with the Purpose. This does not restrict:

**(a)** general advertising or recruitment campaigns not specifically directed at the other Party's personnel; or

**(b)** the engagement of any person who applies on their own initiative in response to such a campaign.

**13.3 Reasonableness.** Each Party acknowledges that the restrictions in this clause are reasonable in scope and duration and are necessary to protect legitimate interests.

## 15. Term, Survival and Return of Materials

**15.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for **three (3) years**, unless terminated earlier by either Party on **thirty (30) days'** written notice.

**15.2 Survival of confidentiality.** The obligations of confidence in this Agreement survive its expiry or termination and continue:

**(a)** for **five (5) years** from the date of each disclosure, in respect of Confidential Information generally;

**(b)** **indefinitely**, for so long as the information remains a Trade Secret, in respect of Trade Secrets, source code, system architecture, data models, algorithms and security information; and

**(c)** **indefinitely**, in respect of any personal data and any information whose disclosure would breach applicable law.

**15.3 Return or destruction.** On expiry or termination, or on the Disclosing Party's written request at any time, the Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within **thirty (30) days**:

**(a)** cease all use of the Confidential Information;

**(b)** return or securely destroy all material containing Confidential Information, including all copies, extracts and Derivative Works, in every medium;

**(c)** permanently delete Confidential Information from all systems, storage and devices under its control, to the extent technically practicable; and

**(d)** certify compliance in writing, signed by an authorised officer, if requested.

**15.4 Retention exceptions.** A Receiving Party may retain:

**(a)** one copy of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation or bona fide internal document-retention or audit policy; and

**(b)** copies held in routine, non-targeted electronic backups made in the ordinary course, which shall not be restored for any purpose other than disaster recovery.

Any Confidential Information so retained remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained, notwithstanding clause 15.2.

**15.5 Credentials.** On expiry or termination, all access credentials issued under clause 10.3 are revoked automatically and shall not be used.

## 16. Remedies and Injunctive Relief

**16.1 Inadequacy of damages.** Each Party acknowledges that Confidential Information and Platform Material are of a special, unique and extraordinary character; that unauthorised use or disclosure would cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy; and that the harm would be difficult to quantify.

**16.2 Injunctive relief.** Accordingly, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek **immediate injunctive relief, specific performance and other equitable relief** to prevent or restrain any actual or threatened breach, **without the necessity of proving actual damages and without the requirement to post any bond or other security**, save where such a requirement is mandatory under applicable law.

**16.3 Cumulative remedies.** The remedies in this Agreement are cumulative and are in addition to any other remedy available.

**16.4 Costs.** In any proceeding to enforce this Agreement, the prevailing Party is entitled to recover its reasonable legal fees, expert fees and costs, to the extent permitted by the law of the forum.

**16.5 Trade secret remedies preserved.** Nothing in this Agreement limits any remedy available under the United States Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, under any applicable state uniform trade secrets legislation, or under any equivalent law of Zimbabwe or any other applicable jurisdiction.

**16.6 Statutory whistleblower notice.** Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a document filed under seal in a legal proceeding. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to limit that protection or to discourage any lawful report to a governmental authority.

## 23. Drafting Notes for Counsel

> **These notes are for the reviewing lawyers and should be removed before the Agreement is circulated for signature.**

**N.1 Governing law and forum (clause 18).** This is the most consequential open item and should be settled by instruction, not by preference. The relevant tension is that Afronovation's principal asset requiring protection is United States intellectual property, while the counterparty is a Zimbabwean statutory body whose assets and personnel are in Zimbabwe. A judgment or award is only as useful as its enforceability against the party that must satisfy it.

Three configurations are commonly used, and counsel should advise on which fits:

| Option | Governing law | Forum | Consideration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Delaware or North Carolina | Arbitration seated in the USA | Strongest for Afronovation on the law; enforcement against a Zimbabwean statutory body may require recognition proceedings in Zimbabwe |
| B | English law | Arbitration seated in London (LCIA) or Paris (ICC) | Neutral third-country forum, familiar to both civil and common-law counterparties; both the USA and Zimbabwe are parties to the New York Convention |
| C | Zimbabwean law | Arbitration seated in Harare | Most acceptable to a government counterparty; weakest position for enforcing US trade secret protections |

Option B is the configuration most often accepted by both sides in government-to-foreign-vendor arrangements. Confirm Zimbabwe's current status and any reservations under the New York Convention before settling.

**N.2 Sovereign immunity (clause 12.5).** A statutory body may or may not be able to waive immunity under Zimbabwean law, and the answer may differ for immunity from suit and immunity from execution. Zimbabwean counsel should confirm ZIDA's capacity to give the waiver and whether any ministerial or Attorney-General concurrence is required for it to bind. If a waiver cannot be given, clause 16 (injunctive relief) should be reconsidered, since its practical value depends on enforceability.

**N.3 Access to information (clause 8).** Confirm the disclosure obligations that apply to ZIDA under Zimbabwean access-to-information and public-records law, and confirm that exemptions for trade secrets and commercially sensitive third-party information are available. If they are not, or are narrow, the categories in Schedule B may need to be marked and handled differently.

**N.4 Data protection (clause 11.6).** Confirm the requirements of the Data Protection Act [Chapter 11:12] in relation to cross-border transfer of personal data to the United States, and whether a separate data processing agreement or transfer mechanism is required before any production deployment.

**N.5 Non-solicitation (clause 13.2).** Enforceability of non-solicitation covenants varies by jurisdiction and, in the United States, by state. Confirm that the twelve-month period is enforceable under the chosen governing law, and consider whether it should be reciprocal in the terms drafted or narrowed on one side.

**N.6 Term and survival (clause 15).** The five-year general survival period with indefinite protection for trade secrets is a common structure. Note that in some civil-law jurisdictions an indefinite obligation may be read down; if a civil-law governing law is chosen under N.1, counsel should consider whether a long fixed period is more robust than an indefinite one.

**N.7 Public visibility (clause 5(iii)).** This provision is deliberately drafted to prevent an argument that because the platform is reachable on the public internet, its design and architecture are in the public domain. Confirm that it is drafted robustly enough under the chosen governing law, as it is central to protecting the asset.

**N.8 Signature authority.** Confirm who within ZIDA has authority to bind the Agency to a confidentiality instrument of this kind, and whether execution requires a common seal, a witness, or the concurrence of a parent ministry. Confirm the same for any additional institutions added to Schedule A.2.

**N.9 Order of operations.** This Agreement is intended to be executed **before** any demonstration, credential issue, or delivery of platform documentation. If material has already been shared, add a recital confirming that prior disclosures are deemed made under and governed by this Agreement, so that the protection is not read as running only from the Effective Date.
